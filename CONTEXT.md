# Stardew Valley Tracker

A checklist for reaching Perfection in Stardew Valley, checked off from any device. Master Angler is the first part built.

## Language

### The game

**Perfection**:
The game's own 100% measure, shown in the Perfection Tracker (1.5+). The tracker's long-term goal.
_Avoid_: 100%, completion, full completion

**Category**:
One of the parts the Perfection Tracker is built from, such as Fish caught, Cooking or Golden Walnuts. Each Category has its own page.
_Avoid_: section, type, tab

**Goal**:
One thing to do on the way to Perfection. Some Goals are simply done or not done (catch a Pufferfish). Others are a number toward a target (Golden Walnuts found, out of 130). Every Goal belongs to one Category. A Fish is one kind of Goal.
_Avoid_: item, task, entry

**Catalog**:
The fixed list of every Goal, as the game defines it.
_Avoid_: database, item list

**Achievement**:
One of the game's named achievements, such as Master Angler. Many Achievements fall out of Perfection along the way; they are noted, not the goal.
_Avoid_: trophy, badge

**Fish**:
An entry in the Fish tab of the game's Collections menu, which is what Master Angler checks. Includes Seaweed, the algae and the jellies; excludes the Extended Family fish.
_Avoid_: catch (as a noun)

**Zone**:
A place you'd go to catch a group of Fish, such as the Beach or the Mines. The tracker's grouping, not the game's.
_Avoid_: location, area

**Bundle**:
A Community Center bundle that asks for particular Fish.

### Your progress

**Farm**:
One Stardew Valley save. Each Farm has its own Progress; a new save is a new Farm.
_Avoid_: save, game, profile, account

**Farm Code**:
The name you choose for a Farm when you start it, which identifies its Progress. No two Farms share a Farm Code. Whoever knows it can view and change that Progress.
_Avoid_: password, key, room code

**Farm Link**:
The web address of the tracker with a Farm Code embedded. Opening it (or the Home Screen app saved from it) shows that Farm.
_Avoid_: invite, share link

**Progress**:
What you have marked done on one Farm. The tracker records what you say, not what the game says.
_Avoid_: save, state, data

**Done**:
A done-or-not Goal you have marked as done on this Farm. For a Fish, Done is called **Caught**.
_Avoid_: owned, collected, checked, complete

**Done Date**:
The real-world date you marked a Goal Done (for a Fish, the Caught date). Unmarking it clears the date.
_Avoid_: timestamp, completion date
